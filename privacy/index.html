Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 (Adapty added)

Hibr is an app for writing on photos and making greeting images. It is built so that your photos and designs never leave your iPhone. Hibr has no servers of its own, no account and no analytics.

Photos and camera

  • Picking a photo. Hibr uses Apple's photo picker, which runs outside the app. Hibr only receives the photo you choose; it cannot see the rest of your library and does not ask for library access.
  • Camera. If you take a photo in Hibr, iOS asks for camera access. The photo is used only in your design.
  • Saving. To save a finished image, iOS asks for permission to add photos. Hibr only adds images; it never reads your library.
  • Editing and cut-out. Filters, adjustments and removing a photo's background run on your iPhone with Apple's Core Image and Vision. Nothing is uploaded.

What is stored on your iPhone

Your designs ("My designs"), the photos you prepared for them, and your settings (default size and fonts, whether you have seen the introduction, how many free cut-outs you used). They stay in Hibr's storage on your iPhone. Deleting a design removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases

Hibr Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Hibr never sees your card or Apple ID details.

Subscriptions are managed with Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.), which checks whether you have an active Pro subscription and lets us change the plans the paywall offers. To do that, Hibr sends Adapty: a random identifier created for this install (not your name, email or Apple ID), your purchase and subscription history (product, price, dates, renewals, refunds), the paywall you saw, and basic device facts (iPhone model, iOS version, app version, country and language). Hibr turns off Adapty's collection of the advertising identifier and of your IP address. Adapty does not receive your photos or designs, and the data is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Crash reports

If the App Store or TestFlight version of Hibr crashes or freezes, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be fixed. A report contains technical details: the app version, the code that was running, iPhone model, iOS version, memory and similar device state, and a random install identifier. It does not contain your photos, your designs or their text, screenshots, a screen recording, your name or email. IP addresses are not stored (Sentry's IP scrubbing is on). About one in ten sessions also sends performance timings for the same purpose. Reports are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared.

What Hibr does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, tracking or advertising identifier. The only analytics are subscription events in Adapty (above).
  • No AI or cloud services: everything is drawn and processed on your iPhone.
  • No access to your location, contacts or microphone.

Links that leave the app

Rate, Share, Feature requests, Support, Terms and this policy open the App Store, the share sheet, your browser or your email app, under those services' own policies. Feature requests are hosted on Canny; anything you post there is public.

Children

Hibr collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
