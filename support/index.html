Frequently asked questions

My Arabic letters look separated.

Every font in Hibr joins Arabic letters. If a word looks wrong, try another font from the Font tab; letter spacing is turned off for Arabic on purpose, so joins stay intact.

Saving says "Can't save to Photos".

Hibr needs permission to add photos. Open Settings › Hibr › Photos and choose "Add Photos Only".

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: two templates per occasion plus twenty everyday ones, three fonts per script, all colours and gradients and twenty images, outline, shadow and box, a basic sticker set, three cut-outs, five saved designs; saved images carry a small watermark. Pro unlocks everything, removes the watermark and adds curved text, lettering and gradient fills.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions at least 24 hours before renewal. On a new iPhone, open Hibr › Settings › Restore purchases.

Where are my designs?

Under "My designs" on the Home screen. They are saved automatically on your iPhone (not in the cloud), so deleting the app deletes them.

Reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Hibr version (Settings, bottom of the screen), what you did and what happened. "Support" in the app's Settings opens an email with the version details filled in.
